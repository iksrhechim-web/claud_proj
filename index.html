$4M+ average global cost of a data breach*

*Figures are approximate and rounded from widely published industry reports. They vary by year and study; use them for general awareness.

Why it matters

The importance of cybersecurity

Banking, healthcare, education, transport and communication all depend on digital systems. A single successful attack can expose personal information, interrupt essential services, or cause serious financial and reputational damage.

Good security is not only about technology. It combines secure systems, well-trained people and clear processes. Small habits, such as using unique passwords and installing updates, prevent a large share of real-world incidents.
