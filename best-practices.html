Practical guidance

Security Best Practices

Good security is built from consistent habits. Find recommendations for your situation, then work through the checklist to see how well protected you are.

Tailored advice

Recommendations by audience

👤 Individuals

  • Use a password manager and unique passwords for every account.
  • Turn on MFA for email, banking and social media.
  • Install updates promptly on phones, computers and smart devices.
  • Be skeptical of unexpected messages, even from known contacts.
  • Review privacy settings and limit what you share publicly.

🎓 Students

  • Protect school and university accounts with MFA, since they are valuable targets.
  • Avoid pirated software and unofficial downloads, which commonly carry malware.
  • Use a VPN or mobile data instead of open Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks.
  • Lock shared and library computers, and log out when finished.
  • Back up coursework to cloud storage and an external drive.

🏪 Small Businesses

  • Enforce MFA and unique passwords across all staff accounts.
  • Keep automatic updates on and maintain a simple asset list.
  • Back up critical data using the 3-2-1 rule and test restores.
  • Train employees to spot phishing and invoice fraud.
  • Limit admin rights and remove accounts of departed staff immediately.

🏢 Large Organizations

  • Adopt a recognized framework such as NIST CSF or ISO 27001.
  • Implement zero-trust principles and strong identity management.
  • Run continuous monitoring with a security operations capability.
  • Conduct regular risk assessments, audits and incident drills.
  • Manage third-party and supply-chain risk.
Core topics

Essential security areas

🔑

Password Security

  • Choose long passphrases rather than complex short ones.
  • Never share passwords or reuse them.
  • Change a password immediately if a service reports a breach.
✉️

Email Safety

  • Check the sender address and hover over links before clicking.
  • Don't open unexpected attachments.
  • Verify unusual requests through a separate, trusted channel.
💻

Device Security

  • Enable screen locks and full-disk encryption.
  • Keep operating systems and apps updated.
  • Install software only from trusted sources.
📶

Network Security

  • Secure home Wi-Fi with WPA3 or WPA2 and a strong passphrase.
  • Change default router credentials.
  • Avoid sensitive activity on open public networks.
🗂️

Data Protection

  • Collect and keep only the data you need.
  • Encrypt sensitive files and backups.
  • Dispose of old devices and media securely.
🚨

Be Ready to Respond

  • Know who to contact if something looks wrong.
  • Keep important contacts and recovery codes accessible offline.
  • Read the Incident Response guide.
